This whole site is generated by Hugo — `tako dev` reruns the build on
every save, and `tako deploy` runs it once and embeds the output in
the service binary. No bucket, no CDN config, no second pipeline.

The comments below are the only dynamic thing on the page: an htmx
island that asks a tako fragment for its HTML.
